Our Work

Much of our work is carried out in confidence for the communities we serve. These examples describe the kinds of work we do in general terms, without identifying details.

Information Management · Geospatial

A GIS-based asset management system

Challenge
A First Nation needed one reliable way to track the condition and maintenance of community infrastructure.
Approach
We designed a GIS-based system, automated its data pipelines and built live dashboards for operations staff.
Outcome
Staff can see asset health in real time and plan maintenance with confidence.

Geospatial · Content

A territorial bioregional atlas

Challenge
A community wanted to bring its knowledge of the territory together in a form it controls and can share with partners.
Approach
We combined community-held knowledge, field-collected data and public datasets, with narrative woven through the maps.
Outcome
A living reference for planning, referrals and teaching.

Strategy

Marine and land use planning support

Challenge
A nation needed a clear, community-led basis for decisions about the use of its lands and waters.
Approach
We supported participatory engagement and brought spatial analysis, policy and planning together.
Outcome
A shared plan the community can stand behind when engaging with other governments.

Geospatial

Terrain mapping from LiDAR

Challenge
An engineering project needed accurate terrain information for a remote site.
Approach
We processed large LiDAR datasets into precise 3D point clouds and elevation models.
Outcome
Reliable terrain data to support design and risk decisions.
