Data Sovereignty

Indigenous data sovereignty is at the root of how we manage information. As data custodians, we work on behalf of communities, never in place of them.

Our commitments

Your data stays yours

Communities own the information about their people, lands and culture. We act as custodians on your behalf, not as owners.

Your rules govern it

Access, storage and sharing follow your community’s own protocols and decisions.

Clear agreements

How information is collected, used and held is agreed up front, so everyone knows where they stand.

Safeguarded for the future

We help keep data secure and usable for generations, and hand everything over at the end of an engagement.

Frameworks we work within

We follow each community’s own data governance protocols and, where a community has adopted them, recognized principles such as OCAP® (Ownership, Control, Access and Possession).
