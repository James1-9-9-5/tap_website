About Us

Tapestry is a values driven organization made up of a team of interdisciplinary professionals committed to facilitating Indigenous stewardship, governance and decision-making. With over 25 years of experience supporting Indigenous communities in British Columbia and across Canada, our work is grounded in humility, respect, and a dedication to weaving together complementary ways of knowing.
