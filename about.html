About Us

Tapestry is a values driven organization made up of a team of interdisciplinary professionals committed to facilitating Indigenous stewardship, governance and decision-making. With over 25 years of experience supporting Indigenous communities in British Columbia and across Canada, our work is grounded in humility, respect, and a dedication to weaving together complementary ways of knowing.

How we work

  • Participatory community engagement shapes every project.
  • We strengthen Indigenous governance by developing robust information management systems and decision-making frameworks.
  • We use technology to enable territorial and community planning and capacity building.

What guides our work

Humility

We listen first, and let each community’s priorities guide the work.

Respect

We respect Indigenous governance and data sovereignty, and support communities in making their own decisions.

Weaving together ways of knowing

We bring Indigenous knowledge and technical expertise together so that each strengthens the other.
