Supporting communities to lead

Tapestry Research Group helps Indigenous communities plan, map, and manage their lands, assets, and information.

Our work is grounded in humility and respect. We weave together complementary ways of knowing, so that the tools and plans we build reflect each community’s own priorities and decision-making.

Our approach

How an engagement works

  1. Listen and scope

    We begin by learning your community’s priorities, existing systems, and capacity, and agree together on goals and on who leads.

  2. Plan together

    We co-design the approach with your team, including how information will be gathered, held, and shared.

  3. Build and deliver

    We carry out the work, whether maps, systems, plans, or reports, with regular check-ins along the way.

  4. Hand over and support

    We document what we build, train your staff, and leave your community able to carry the work forward.

Data sovereignty

Your information, your rules

Indigenous data sovereignty is at the root of how we manage information. As data custodians, we work on behalf of communities, never in place of them.

We follow each community’s own data governance protocols, as well as recognized principles such as OCAP® (Ownership, Control, Access, and Possession).

{% include icons/key.svg %}

Your data stays yours

Communities own the information about their people, lands, and culture. We act as custodians, not owners.

{% include icons/checklist.svg %}

Your protocols govern it

Access, storage, and sharing follow your community’s own protocols and decisions.

{% include icons/agreement.svg %}

Clear agreements

How information is collected, used, and held is agreed up front, so everyone knows where they stand.

{% include icons/shield.svg %}

Safeguarded for the future

We keep data secure and usable for generations, and hand everything over at the end of an engagement.
